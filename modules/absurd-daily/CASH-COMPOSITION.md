# CASH full-owner composition candidate

This local increment starts at `f0f348838a762e481a0121ca1ff96657320ef6a3`.
It does not publish, deploy, merge, enable a feature, or constitute acceptance.

CASH now gives the real native/light renderer the entire bounded game owner.
Its former bordered viewport and cream controls dock are removed. Sorting
controls occupy opposite table edges; amount, classified-card count and mistakes
float over the scene. The same owner retains share/fullscreen, pause, help,
restart, explicit renderer choice, completed-run capture and export recovery.
Short visible labels have the original complete accessible action names.
The full current-card description and last outcome remain in help and the live
accessible status. Start/recovery copy scrolls independently of its actions.

Portrait CASH fits its original 3.2 × 2.15 world card between the HUD and sorting
controls. Landscape retains its 42° projection. Both renderers, moving-card
contact and drag displacement continue to use the shared camera. No money art,
mesh, material, deck, cooldown, 48px swipe threshold, scoring or export facts
are replaced. PHOTO/STAMP composition styles and meshes are untouched.

## Two-action recovery correction after 450f40bc

Independent source review found that a completed capture failure plus renderer
loss could clip both enlarged recovery actions in short landscape. The two-action
CASH sheet now uses the full inner owner below the retained top toolbar. Copy
and nonshrinking actions share one start-aligned scrolling surface; the ordinary
single-action and playing composition stays unchanged. A new browser regression
creates capture failure and real context loss, then enlarges the newly created
actions to 200%, checks scroll/hit/text/focus reachability in normal phone and
landscape owners, and recovers the original completed facts through light mode.
The regression is authored for later authorized execution, not browser proof.
